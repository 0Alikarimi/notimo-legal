## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Notimo work?
Notimo helps you learn to read sheet music through interactive lessons, levels, and real-time piano practice. You can play with a MIDI keyboard, your iPhone touchscreen, or by singing into the microphone.

### What's included in the free version?
- 30 levels (15 Methodik + 15 Technik)
- Free Practice mode in C major
- Music theory lessons
- Free Piano

### What does Notimo Pro unlock?
- All 340+ levels in the learning path
- Advanced practice modes (intervals, chords, scales)
- All key signatures in custom practice
- All theme colors

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID:
**iPhone Settings → Apple ID (top) → Subscriptions → Notimo Pro → Cancel Subscription**

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep Pro access until then.

### I bought Pro but it's not unlocked
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall. If that doesn't work, contact us at the email above.

### MIDI keyboard not working?
1. Make sure your MIDI keyboard is connected (Lightning/USB-C cable or Bluetooth)
2. Open Notimo Settings → MIDI → Diagnose
3. Press a key on your keyboard — Notimo should show the note
